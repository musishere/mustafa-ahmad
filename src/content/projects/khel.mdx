## Problem

Booking a turf in Lahore, Karachi or Islamabad still runs on phone calls, WhatsApp groups and cash. When a group plays, one person usually pays and then chases everyone else for their share.

## What players get

- **Book a turf in the app.** Find a nearby turf, see open slots and send a booking request. The turf owner approves it, and the slot is yours.
- **Split the cost.** The organiser pays their share to lock the slot, and friends get a request to pay theirs through JazzCash, EasyPaisa or Raast. Nobody has to chase anyone for cash.
- **Find a match.** Players get a skill rating from their results, and matchmaking pairs them with nearby players at their level.
- **Keep playing.** Badges and weekly streaks reward regular games.

## What turf owners get

A business account to list their turf, approve bookings and see who's coming, in place of a phone and a notebook.
